# Reflow Post-v1.0 Product Roadmap

This document captures deferred feature requests, advanced architectural enhancements, and future ecosystem opportunities identified during the Reflow v1.0 release freeze.

---

## 1. Advanced Multi-Tenant & SaaS Capabilities
- **Organization Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Custom granular permissions for enterprise teams (e.g. `Editor`, `Reviewer`, `Publisher`).
- **SaaS Billing & Metering:** Self-hosted usage reporting and billing gateway integrations (Stripe/LemonSqueezy).

## 2. Platform Integrations & Connectors
- **Direct Native Instagram Reels & TikTok Uploads:** Additional official OAuth2 publishing workflows beyond current mock connectors.
- **Podcast RSS Feed Auto-Ingestion:** Scheduled background polling for RSS audio feeds.

## 3. Advanced AI & Media Features
- **Speaker Diarization:** Multi-speaker identification in long-form video transcriptions.
- **Custom Font Uploads for Burn-in Captions:** Support for uploading `.ttf`/`.woff2` files for FFmpeg subtitle rendering.

## 4. Mobile & Desktop Companions
- **Native iOS/Android Review App:** Mobile approval companion for on-the-go publication sign-offs.
